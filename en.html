Kissa Merge Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "Kissa Merge" (Japanese title: かさねて純喫茶; the "App") handles user information.

1. Information the App collects

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. There is no account registration.

The following information is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer:

  • Your game progress, such as items on the board, orders, coins, energy, and shop level
  • Records of the items you have discovered in the Menu Book
  • Settings such as sound effects and vibration

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To deliver ads, Google may collect and use the following information:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking in your device settings
  • Device information such as IP address, device model, and OS version
  • Usage information such as how many times ads are shown and whether they are tapped

This information is used to deliver ads, measure their effectiveness, prevent fraud, and show ads that are relevant to you. For details on how Google handles information, please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Tracking permission and how to change it

After you serve your first order, the App asks for permission to track through your iPhone or iPad. If you do not allow it, you can still use the App normally, and you will see ads that are not personalized.

You can change this at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can also open it from "Open Tracking Settings" in the App's settings screen.

Users in the UK and Switzerland can change their ad consent at any time from "Change Ad Consent" in the App's settings screen.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases such as coins, Energy Cans, menus, and Remove Ads are processed through Apple's App Store. Payment information is managed by Apple and is never received by the developer.

To avoid giving the same purchase twice, the App stores purchase transaction IDs on your device. This information is also never sent to the developer.

5. Sharing information with third parties

The developer does not sell or provide user information to third parties, except for the information collected by the advertising service (Google) described above and where required by law.

6. Children

The App is not primarily directed at children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.
