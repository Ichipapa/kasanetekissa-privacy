FAQ

How do I play?

Tap "Restock" to get an item on the board. Drag two matching items together to make the next item (Coffee Beans → Ground Coffee → Drip Coffee …).

When you have the items shown in an order at the top, tap "Serve". Serving earns coins and gives back a little energy.

Save up coins to expand your shop. Expanding makes your board bigger and increases order rewards and maximum energy.

I ran out of energy

Energy recovers by 1 every minute, and some comes back right away each time you serve an order. You can also watch an ad to refill it (limited times per day).

My board is full

Merge matching items to free up space, or drag items you don't need to "Sell" in the bottom right to turn them into coins.

What are "Shop Earnings"?

Your shop keeps earning coins while the app is closed, based on your shop level (up to 8 hours). You can collect them the next time you open the app.

My menus or Remove Ads disappeared after changing devices

Tap "Restore Purchases" in the settings screen (gear icon in the top right). You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy them.

Note: Coins and Energy Cans are used up when spent, so they can't be restored.
Note: Game progress is saved on your device and does not carry over to a new device.

I bought coins but they didn't arrive

If your connection is unstable, it may take a moment. Please close and reopen the app. If they still don't appear, email us with the date and time of your purchase.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple. Please use Apple's "Report a Problem" page.
